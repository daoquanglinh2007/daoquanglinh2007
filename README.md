### Hi, I'm Linh 👋

I study Computer Science and Mathematics at [NUS](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/), and I grew up in Hanoi doing competitive programming. I still think in algorithms first, but these days I mostly build backend services and LLM agents.

- 🧪 I co-built **[Repro](https://github.com/phuchd210406/BuckyAgent)**, an agent that turns a vague bug report ("the checkout is broken") into a failing pytest test, and won't accept a patch until that test passes. It runs Claude on Amazon Bedrock, and the demo replays recorded model calls, so it runs offline in under a minute. Try it on [repro-playground](https://github.com/daoquanglinh2007/repro-playground), a small app with seven planted bugs.
- 🎹 I co-built **[Pianus Studio](https://pianus-studio-orbital26.vercel.app/)**, a piano you play with your computer keyboard, for NUS Orbital (Apollo 11). I worked on the FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend and the recorder that replays your notes with exact timing.
- ♟️ My [CodeCup 2023 bot](https://github.com/daoquanglinh2007/entropy-codecup2023) for the game Entropy uses expectiminimax with alpha-beta pruning and placed 8th of 75.
- 📚 **[competitive-programming](https://github.com/daoquanglinh2007/competitive-programming)** holds all 2,510 of my submissions to 1,131 problems, sorted by topic and difficulty.
- 🏆 I represented Vietnam at **APIO 2025**, won First Prize at the Vietnam National Olympiad in Informatics 2024 and the ICPC Asia Hanoi high-school title, and I'm a **[Codeforces Master](https://codeforces.com/profile/daoquanglinh2k7)** (max 2294) and **[AtCoder 1 Dan](https://atcoder.jp/users/daoquanglinh2007)**.
- 👨‍🏫 I'm a teaching assistant for CS1101S at NUS. Before that I taught about 40 high-school students advanced algorithms and wrote 24 original problems for public contests.
